{% endblock %} {% block help_title %}How Strategy works{% endblock %} {% block help %}

Strategy in HQ has two layers so the team does not accidentally maintain conflicting documents.

1. Master roadmap

The external Gravitas Roadmap is the canonical strategic document. If strategy changes, update it there first.

2. HQ objectives and working notes

Objectives translate strategy into outcomes that projects can support. Working notes are for decisions, principles or context that the team needs during execution.

Do not create a document when…

  • It is just a task — put it in Projects.
  • It is content research — put it in Research.
  • It is a script or editorial workflow — put it in Content Studio.
A good strategy section stays small. If it becomes a document dump, the team stops trusting it.
