The Research section is the shared source library for Gravitas. It should reduce repeated searching and make scientific review easier.

What belongs here?

  • Peer-reviewed papers
  • Datasets
  • Books
  • Reports and strong reference articles
  • Interviews or videos used as a source

What should notes contain?

Record why the source matters, its important caveats, and what part of the project it informs. Avoid copying a long abstract that nobody will read.

Claims and evidence

The data model can connect a claim to sources as Supports, Challenges or Context. The next Evidence Map interface will make those relationships visual inside Content Studio.

A citation is not a quality guarantee. Scientific review should still consider methodology, relevance, limitations and whether the source really supports the claim being made.
