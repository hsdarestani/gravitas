Before production starts

The central question, brief and evidence should be strong enough that the team agrees what the piece is trying to explain and what it will not claim.

Scientific review

Use scientific notes to record caveats, disputed claims and required revisions. “Approved” should mean the argument is defensible, not simply that someone read it.

Files and video

Do not upload raw video to the Gravitas application server. Keep large media on Frame.io, Drive, YouTube, Vimeo or object storage and create an Asset reference.

After publishing

Use Distribution for channel-specific execution and Post-mortem to record what the team learned from audience behaviour and production friction.

The CMS item is the public output. Content Studio is the internal record of how that output was researched, produced and reviewed.
