Nextcloud is the default internal file workspace. Put documents, PDFs, research packs, images and other normal team files there, then attach the Nextcloud link to the relevant HQ project or content item.

What goes where?

NextcloudDefault for team files, shared documents, research material and normal project assets.
Frame.ioOptional specialist review/versioning for video.
YouTube / VimeoPublished or streamed video.
R2 / S3 / video serverLarge-scale object storage or course/raw video when needed.

Server rule

Do not turn the Gravitas application disk into a video archive. HQ stores references; Nextcloud handles normal files; heavy video gets dedicated storage.
