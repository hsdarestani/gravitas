{% endblock %} {% block help_title %}How to use Overview{% endblock %} {% block help %}

Overview is not a reporting dashboard. It is the place to decide what you should do next.

Use it in this order

  1. Check overdue work and projects that need attention.
  2. Open your next assigned task.
  3. Look at content waiting for review or production.
  4. Use Search when you already know what you are looking for.

What belongs elsewhere?

Strategy decisions belong under Strategy, execution under Projects, editorial work under Content Studio, evidence under Research, and files as external references under Assets.

HQ is designed around one rule: every important piece of work should have an owner, a status and a place where the team can find its context.
