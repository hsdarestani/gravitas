The goal is not to fill every field. The goal is to leave enough durable context that the team can act without relying on private memory or chat history.

Good titles

Use concrete names that still make sense in a list six months from now. Avoid titles like “New thing”, “Research”, or “Fix this”.

Ownership

An owner is accountable for moving the record forward. It does not mean they must do every task themselves.

Descriptions

Explain the outcome, important constraints and what “done” means. Keep execution details in tasks rather than turning the description into a checklist.

If a field does not help a teammate make a decision or perform work, leaving it blank is better than adding filler.
