Content Studio is not a generic CMS. It manages the internal work required before something deserves to be published.

The stages

  1. Idea: interesting question, not yet committed.
  2. Topic score: decide whether it is worth the team’s time.
  3. Brief: audience, angle, promise, scope and structure.
  4. Evidence map: claims are connected to credible sources.
  5. Script: narrative draft.
  6. Scientific review: challenge claims, caveats and framing.
  7. Production: recording, edit, graphics and media.
  8. Companion page: website material, sources and interactive elements.
  9. Distribution: YouTube, Shorts, newsletter and community.
  10. Post-mortem: record what the team learned.
Do not move a piece forward just because time passed. The stage should communicate what is actually complete.
