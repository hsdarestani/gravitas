Challenge

Taylor claims the protein becomes more stable as temperature increases. Try changing the assumed transition midpoint. What happens to the predicted curve? Can the four observations alone justify a claim about 60–70 °C?

Scientific answer: No. An extrapolation is not a measurement. The model's assumptions and new observations must be tested independently.

Method & limitations

The engine computes equilibrium folded fraction as 1 / (1 + exp((T − Tm) / width)) and illustrative time relaxation from the initial state at 20 °C. Practice observations are generated by the same model with fixed small offsets at a 10-minute baseline.

This teaching simulation omits real amino-acid sequence, solvent, reversibility, kinetic intermediates, errors and true molecular dynamics. Exported JSON contains parameters and outputs so the numerical example can be reproduced.
