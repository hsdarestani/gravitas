01 · The First Path

Eight Steps, in Order

Each step builds on the one before it. Take them in order, or stop at the one you need.

Path 01 · AI in Research · Open now

From what a model is doing, to using one on your own data

From what these models actually do, to using one on your own data without fooling yourself. No machine learning background assumed; some statistics helps.

8 steps · ~6 hours

Start the Path
  1. 01

    What a model is doing when it answers

    The mechanism, without the mysticism: prediction over a learned distribution, and what that rules out.

  2. 02

    Where the productivity actually is

    Triage, code and drafting versus deciding what is worth asking. The second one has not moved.

  3. 03

    Retrieval, and why citations get invented

    Why a fluent system produces plausible references, and what grounding does and does not fix.

  4. 04

    Evaluating a claim you cannot inspect

    Held-out sets, ablations, and the questions to ask when the mechanism is unavailable.

  5. 05

    Using a model on your own data

    A worked example end to end, including the two places it quietly goes wrong.

  6. 06

    Reproducibility with a stochastic tool

    Seeds, versions and the parts of your pipeline that are now a moving target.

  7. 07

    The ethics you will actually meet

    Authorship, disclosure, review, and what your funder already requires.

  8. 08

    Where this breaks

    The open problems, honestly stated, including the ones we argued about on camera.

03 · How a Path Works

Free From the First Step

A path asks for no account and no payment. Join when you want to argue with what you read.

  1. 01

    Follow

    Take the steps in order. Each is labelled with what it is: a video, an essay, a notebook, a checklist.

  2. 02

    Read Whole

    No paywall and no locked steps. Every step is open from the first one.

  3. 03

    Talk It Through

    Bring what a step raised to the community, where every Topic keeps its own discussion.
