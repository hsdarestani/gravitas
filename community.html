Community

Watching is one role among several. The point of a member account is that your contribution is visible and actually changes what gets made.

Take part as

Roles

Reader

Comment, question and argue. The lowest-friction role, and the one that shapes most dossiers.

Researcher

Bring evidence, correct us, and flag where a claim outruns its source.

Translator

Carry a dossier into another language, with the nuance intact.

Critic

Write the counter-argument. We publish the strongest one we receive.

Builder

Prototype games, simulations and tools for the lab.

Host

Run a study group, a reading session or a live discussion.

Thought experiment

The best arguments get taken apart in next week's video.

How it works

Every Monday a new situation. Wednesday the votes close. Friday the reasoning — including the arguments that changed our minds — goes into a video or an essay.

What members do

Nine ways to take part

  • Propose a dossier subject and vote on what gets made next
  • Join a study group or an online session
  • Send questions for upcoming interview guests
  • Take part in collective experiments and data collection
  • Share a project, paper or tool worth attention
  • Write a critique or a response
  • Help translate a dossier
  • Build a game or a tool for the lab
  • Host a discussion or an event

Why bother

Because a media project that only broadcasts eventually runs out of things to say. The dossier list, the guest questions and half the lab ideas come from members.
