Inspect the calculation

The coefficients come from ordinary least squares on four synthetic records. For transparency the axes do not rescale when the selected model changes. Predictions outside a plausible 0–100% range are not silently clipped in the exported numerical results.

Challenge

What is the folded fraction at 70 °C? Compare the linear and quadratic fits. Both have seen exactly the same four data points. Is either prediction a verified result?

No. Changing the mathematical assumption changes the extrapolation. New measurements are required to test it.

Scientific integrity

A graph produced with code is reproducible only when its inputs, processing method, uncertainty meaning and model limitations are disclosed. Making a line smooth does not make its claims true.
