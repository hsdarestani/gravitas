What works here, and where the limits are

Available here: nine adjustable models, twelve prepared experiments, A/B comparisons, a session learning replay, and optional free-text questions to the existing Pulsar service. Prepared explanations are labeled local. Live answers carry a separate model reference and can be mistaken or unavailable.

These are bounded teaching models, not complete courses or empirical forecasts. Learning outcomes require evaluation with educators and learners. Instructor dashboards, LMS integration and institutional deployment are potential pilot scope, not features of this demonstration.

How this approach relates to established learning tools

Simulation-based inquiry and guided tutoring already have strong examples. Our focus is an inspectable loop: a learner’s prediction, one controlled change, the resulting values and a conversation about that evidence in one place. This is our product direction, not a claim of exclusive capabilities or proven superiority.
