# Open questions

These should remain visibly unresolved in the Research Pack.

## OQ01 — Can scalable verification keep up with scalable generation?
Formal proof and executable software have relatively cheap machine verification. Wet-lab science often does not.

## OQ02 — How should scientific benchmarks reward abstention?
If a model is uncertain, what scoring rule makes “I do not know” rational without allowing trivial refusal?

## OQ03 — What is independent verification when all agents share models or training data?
A second model may reproduce the same systematic error.

## OQ04 — How do we measure scientific novelty rather than textual novelty?
Formal correctness does not establish that a result is new or important.

## OQ05 — Can provenance be made default?
Can future research agents automatically retain every source, transformation, tool call, model version and decision needed for audit?

## OQ06 — What is the right unit of replication for agentic research?
The paper? The prompt? The model version? The tool environment? The whole trajectory?

## OQ07 — How much mechanistic understanding is necessary?
The answer may differ across pure prediction, intervention, engineering and theory.

## OQ08 — Does AI produce epistemic monoculture?
Current observational evidence is provocative but not sufficient to settle causal mechanisms.

## OQ09 — How do journal policies work in practice?
Policies are changing faster than robust evidence about their effectiveness.

## OQ10 — How should responsibility be assigned in multi-agent science?
If one agent searches, another writes code, another interprets results and a human approves the final manuscript, where exactly does accountability sit?

## OQ11 — What happens when AI-generated work becomes training data for future scientific AI?
Feedback loops could amplify both knowledge and error.

## OQ12 — Can scientific slop be measured without creating a new gameable metric?
Any quality score can become an optimization target.
