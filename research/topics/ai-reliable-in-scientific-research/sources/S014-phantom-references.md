# S014 — Phantom References

## Scope
Audits accepted camera-ready papers from ICLR, ICML, NeurIPS and USENIX Security.

## Conservative definition
Focuses on identity-level citation failures such as non-existent works and substantial author-list mismatch rather than ordinary metadata drift.

## Editorial use
Useful evidence that citation-integrity failures can survive peer review.

## Caution
Preprint and domain-specific conference corpus. Do not generalize prevalence to all science.
