# S009 — Automated Conjecture Resolution

## Reported architecture
- Rethlas: informal reasoning agent
- Matlas: theorem search
- Archon: formal verification agent
- LeanSearch: formal theorem search
- Lean 4: machine-checkable proof environment

## Reported result
Resolution of an open problem in commutative algebra with the resulting proof formally verified in Lean 4 and essentially no human involvement.

## Editorial caution
Preprint status must remain explicit.

Formal verification checks correctness of the formal object under encoded assumptions; it does not automatically establish novelty, significance or semantic fidelity to an informal problem statement.
