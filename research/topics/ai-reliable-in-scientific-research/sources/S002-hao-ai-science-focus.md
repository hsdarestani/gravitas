# S002 — Hao et al. 2026

## Dataset
41.3 million research papers across the natural sciences.

## Reported associations
The paper reports that scientists engaged in AI-augmented research:
- publish 3.02× more papers;
- receive 4.84× more citations;
- become project leaders 1.37 years earlier.

At the collective level it reports:
- 4.63% contraction in the volume of scientific topics studied;
- 22% decrease in scientists’ engagement with one another.

## Editorial use
Strong evidence for a possible tension between individual benefit and collective exploration.

## Limitation
Observational association. Avoid causal wording such as “AI causes narrowing.”
