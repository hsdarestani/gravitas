# S006 — AlphaFold experimental limits

## Key finding
Many predictions are remarkably accurate, but high-confidence predictions can still differ from experimental density. The authors recommend treating predictions as valuable hypotheses and preserving experimental verification for structural details.

## Important nuance
AlphaFold does not fully represent ligands, covalent modifications, environmental conditions or all conformational states.

## Editorial use
Critical counterweight to “AI prediction replaced the experiment.”
