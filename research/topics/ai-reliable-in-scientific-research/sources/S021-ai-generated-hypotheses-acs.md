# S021 — AI-generated hypotheses and autonomous discovery

## Why retained
Provides a non-Nature scientific perspective on connecting AI hypothesis generation, agentic systems and self-driving laboratories.

## Key idea
The paper proposes a trajectory toward Hypothesis-Driven Autonomous Discovery Systems that connect hypothesis generation with automated experimental validation.

## Important challenge
Scalable metrics for novelty, plausibility, usefulness and actionability remain difficult.

## Editorial use
Useful in open questions and the transition from hypothesis generation to closed-loop science.
