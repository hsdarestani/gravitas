# S008 — Co-Scientist

## System
Multi-agent AI for structured scientific thinking and hypothesis generation.

## Reported process
Agents generate, critique, rank and refine hypotheses with test-time scaling.

## Evidence
The paper reports biomedical cases with experimental validation.

## Editorial caution
Do not equate successful validation cases with general autonomous-scientist reliability across domains.
