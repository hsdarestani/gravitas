# S013 — Hallucinations in the wild

## Corpus
The preprint reports an audit of 111 million references across 2.5 million papers from arXiv, bioRxiv, SSRN and PubMed Central.

## Reported result
The authors report a sharp rise in non-existent references after widespread LLM adoption and provide a conservative estimate of 146,932 hallucinated citations in 2025.

## Editorial caution
This is a preprint. The global number should not be used as a settled fact without additional independent validation.

Use mainly to motivate the scale and auditability of citation integrity.
