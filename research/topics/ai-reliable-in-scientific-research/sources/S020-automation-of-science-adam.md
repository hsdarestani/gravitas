# S020 — Robot Scientist Adam

## Why retained
Important historical counterweight to the idea that autonomous scientific workflows began with LLM agents.

## Reported contribution
Robot Scientist “Adam” autonomously generated functional-genomics hypotheses about yeast and experimentally tested them using laboratory automation.

## Editorial use
Use in the timeline and in discussions of closed-loop research.

## Reliability lesson
Automation of hypothesis generation and experiment existed before modern LLMs; current systems change generality, interface and scale.
