# S019 — Schmidt & Lipson 2009

## Why retained
Historical primary case demonstrating machine discovery of compact physical relationships from experimental motion data.

## Reported contribution
The system automatically searched experimental data and recovered analytical relationships including conservation laws without prior knowledge of physics, kinematics or geometry.

## Editorial use
Shows that machine participation in scientific discovery predates modern generative AI.
