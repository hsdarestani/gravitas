# S010 — Nature AI policy

## Core current principles
- human accountability is non-transferable;
- AI may support but not replace scholarly judgement;
- transparency is required;
- confidentiality and data protection are mandatory;
- risk level depends on function, not simply tool type.

## Important examples of not-permitted use
The policy identifies opaque or unverifiable AI use that replaces accountable human contribution as unacceptable, including delegation of peer review or presenting AI-generated core reasoning as human-derived without disclosure.
