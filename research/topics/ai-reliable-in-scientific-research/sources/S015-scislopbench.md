# S015 — SciSlopBench

## Why retained
Very recent attempt to operationalize scientific slop as failures across Structure, Argument and Artifacts instead of relying on surface AI-writing detection.

## Reported benchmark
390 AI-generated papers paired with human-written papers matched by research problem and contribution type.

## Editorial use
Supports the claim that scientific quality checking must inspect reasoning and artifacts, not only prose fingerprints.

## Caution
Extremely recent preprint. Treat as emerging evidence.
