# S001 — Kalai et al. 2026

## Why retained
Core source for the argument that hallucination is partly shaped by evaluation incentives rather than only by model capability.

## Key result
The paper argues that standard accuracy-oriented evaluation can encourage models to guess instead of abstaining when uncertain.

## Editorial use
Use to justify:
- explicit abstention;
- uncertainty-aware evaluation;
- caution against treating benchmark accuracy as a complete reliability measure.

## Limitation
Do not turn the result into “benchmarks cause hallucination” universally. The paper addresses a specific incentive structure.
