# S016 — Physics-informed ML survey

## Why retained
Supports the methodological bridge between purely data-driven prediction and scientific structure.

## Core framing
Physics-informed ML can incorporate known physical constraints to improve plausibility, data efficiency and generalizability in some settings.

## Editorial caution
Do not present physics-informed methods as universally superior. Benefits depend on domain, constraint quality and task.
