# S017 — The AI Scientist

## Why retained
Direct primary evidence that agentic systems can automate a large portion of the machine-learning research cycle from idea generation through experiments, manuscript production and automated review.

## Reported result
The Nature paper reports that an AI-generated manuscript passed the first round of peer review for a workshop of a top-tier machine-learning conference. The workshop acceptance rate was 70%.

## Important limitation
The domain is machine-learning research, where experiments can be run computationally and cheaply relative to wet-lab sciences.

The paper itself warns that autonomous systems could tax already overwhelmed review systems and add noise to scientific literature.

## Editorial use
Strong case for why scalable generation makes verification capacity a first-class research-infrastructure problem.
