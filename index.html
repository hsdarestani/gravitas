04 · The essay

Reading a number is not evidence

One essay that reads two ways. Overview or In depth: neither is a teaser, both are the whole case.

Read the Essay

OverviewIn depth

The dangerous answer from an AI assistant is not the obviously wrong one. It is the one that looks right: the expected format, a plausible number, a calm tone, and nothing on its surface to say whether anyone checked it.

The short answer

Trust it the way you would trust a capable new colleague: enough to ask, never enough to skip the check on anything that compares, carries a unit, or cites something you did not give it.

07 · Viewpoints

Two readings, both defensible

We publish the strongest case against our own reading. If the counter-argument is weak, that is our failure, and you can say so.

Where Do You Land?

For checking

An assistant changes how fast drafts arrive, not who answers for them. A number in a report is the scientist’s claim, whoever produced it.

The position the video leans toward

Against

If the assistant is right more often than a tired researcher at the end of a long week, a rule that its answers need checking is ritual, not rigour. Checks belong where errors are likely.

The strongest objection we could find
