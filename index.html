01 · THE QUESTION

Is the universe computable?

If the world runs on rules, something ought to be able to run them. Gödel, chaos and thermodynamics all disagree about how far that goes.

03 · VIEWPOINTS

Two readings, both defensible

We publish the strongest case against our own reading. If the counter-argument is weak here, that is our failure.

Where do you land?

For computability

Every physical process we have examined turns out to be simulable given enough resources. “Enough resources” is an engineering complaint, not a metaphysical one.

The position the video leans toward

Against

Undecidability is not a resource complaint: no amount of compute settles it. And treating the universe as executing a computation smuggles in a substrate and a clock no physics requires.
