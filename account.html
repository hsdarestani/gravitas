What an account is for

  • Follow a dossier. They keep growing after publication; you get told what changed rather than having to check.
  • Keep your place in a path. Learning paths run over weeks, not one sitting.
  • Put your name to an argument. Critiques and answers to the weekly question are attributed, and the strongest become part of the next piece.
  • Nothing else. No feed, no ranking, no attention metrics.

Reading, watching, playing and the whole archive stay open without an account. This only exists for the parts where being someone matters.
