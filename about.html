About Gravitas+

A media project, an interactive lab and a community, all of it about how research actually gets made, in every field, and about the questions science keeps raising against itself.

Curiosity brings people in. Capability is what lets them stay. So the aim is not only to explain, but to hand over the tools and the confidence to ask a sharper question and take part in the argument.

Every Piece Tries to Do Four Things

Deepen: show that something apparently simple has layers.
Clarify: make something complicated understandable without talking down.
Connect: link science to philosophy, history, society and technology.
Engage: move you from watching to thinking, testing or arguing.

The subjects rotate; the questions do not. What counts as evidence, how a hypothesis earns its keep, who pays and what that buys, what peer review really catches. A haematologist, a climate modeller and a labour economist are all downstream of the same arguments. Examples get drawn from wherever they are clearest, which is often not physics.

Where We Are Starting

AI and machine learning in scientific research. Not which tool to use, but what happens to the idea of a hypothesis, of explanation, and of expertise when a machine is in the loop.
