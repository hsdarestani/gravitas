2. General information about data processing

We process personal data only where necessary to operate Gravitas+, provide requested features, communicate with users, protect the security of the service, or where you have given consent. The legal bases include, in particular, Article 6(1)(a), (b), (c), and (f) GDPR.

3. Hosting and server log data

Gravitas+ is operated on server infrastructure provided by STRATO. When you access the website, technically necessary connection data may be processed, including your IP address, time of access, requested URL, referrer, browser or user-agent information, and technical error and security data.

This processing is carried out to provide the service securely and reliably and to detect or prevent misuse on the basis of Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Log data is retained only for as long as necessary for operations, troubleshooting, and security, unless legal obligations require a longer retention period.

4. User account and login

If you create an account, we process data including your name, email address, a securely stored password hash, account and session data, and timestamps relating to relevant account activity. This processing is necessary to provide your account and authenticated features and is generally based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Passwords are not stored in plain text. Account data is generally retained until the account is deleted, unless legal retention obligations or legitimate security interests require otherwise.

5. Newsletter and system emails

For the newsletter, we process your email address, confirmation status, and technical records required for the double opt-in procedure. The newsletter is sent only after the corresponding consent has been provided. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. You may withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future.

Transactional emails, such as messages relating to account security, confirmation, or password recovery, are sent where necessary to provide the requested functions. Gravitas+ uses the mail infrastructure configured with STRATO for email delivery.

6. Community, comments, and moderation

When you submit posts or replies in the community, we process the submitted content, its association with your user account, timestamps, and moderation status. Published contributions may be publicly visible together with the display name you use. This processing supports the provision and moderation of community features on the basis of Article 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR.

7. Interactive Lab and learning progress

For signed-in users, progress, results, status values, and related timestamps from interactive Lab features may be stored so that results remain available within the user account. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

8. Technically necessary cookies and local storage

Technically necessary cookies are used for login, session protection, and CSRF protection. A local browser setting may also be stored to preserve a display preference selected by you. This storage is not used for advertising or profiling.

At present, we do not use external advertising or visitor-analytics services, and we do not load Google Fonts from external servers. Non-essential tracking cookies are currently not used.

9. Recipients and service providers

Where necessary, personal data may be shared with technical service providers that supply hosting, server, or email infrastructure for Gravitas+. Personal data is not shared for advertising purposes and is not sold.

10. Backups

Regular backups of website and database content are created to support recovery after technical failures or security incidents. Backups are used only for recovery, security, and operational purposes and are deleted or overwritten in accordance with the applicable retention rules.

11. Your rights

Subject to the requirements of the GDPR, you have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection. Where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time with effect for the future.

To exercise your rights, contact webmaster@gravitasplus.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority.

12. Automated decision-making

We currently do not use solely automated decision-making that produces legal effects or similarly significant effects.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We update this Privacy Policy when features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The current version is always available on this page.
